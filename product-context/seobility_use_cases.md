# 4. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) with Seobility
**URL:** https://www.seobility.net/en/generative-engine-optimization-tool/
**Meta title:** Generative Engine Optimization Tool: Seobility's GEO features

## Overview
Get found in AI-generated answers and classic search results with Seobility's all-in-one SEO tool.

## What GEO means in Seobility's framing
GEO focuses on getting found in AI-generated answers, while SEO focuses on classic search results (Google, Bing). Seobility treats GEO as part of an all-in-one SEO approach targeting both.

## Key use cases

**Optimize website for AI and traditional crawlers**
AI-powered search engines need well-structured, technically sound websites to provide accurate answers. Website Audit scans for technical and on-page issues that could confuse AI or traditional crawlers:
- Detect crawlability and indexing problems
- Improve content accessibility and structure
- Optimize on-page SEO elements like headings for semantic clarity

**Strengthen AI visibility through backlink profile**
Backlinks contribute to domain authority, which increases chances of being featured in AI-generated answers. Backlink Monitoring covers:
- Track new, lost, and broken backlinks
- Analyze link quality
- Identify custom link-building opportunities

**Semantic content optimization for AI search**
Generative AI tools prioritize semantically rich, topic-relevant content when generating answers. TF\*IDF tool helps optimize content for better semantic alignment:
- Discover terms used by top-ranking pages for target keywords
- Optimize content relevance
- Increase chances of being cited by AI tools

## Three-pillar GEO approach
1. Technical SEO (Website Audit) — ensure site is crawlable and well-structured for AI bots
2. Backlink authority (Backlink Monitoring) — build/maintain strong link profile to increase citation likelihood
3. Semantic content (TF\*IDF) — optimize for topic relevance so AI systems recognize and cite the content

## Plan availability
GEO features included in all paid plans. Capabilities vary by plan.

## FAQ highlights
- GEO vs. SEO: GEO = AI-generated answer visibility; SEO = classic search results; Seobility targets both simultaneously
- Website Audit detects crawlability and indexing problems affecting AI visibility and explains how to fix them
- How to get cited in ChatGPT etc.: technically sound site + strong backlinks + semantically optimized content
- Semantically rich content: covers important subtopics, uses natural language, shows how concepts are connected — helps users and AI understand the meaning and purpose of the content
- TF\*IDF for GEO: discovers semantic terms used by top-ranking pages, improves content relevance, increases citation chances by AI tools
- Backlink Monitoring supports GEO: tracks new/lost/broken backlinks, evaluates quality, identifies link-building opportunities
- Free trial: 14 days, all Premium features including GEO tools; payment method required, no charge if cancelled before trial ends
