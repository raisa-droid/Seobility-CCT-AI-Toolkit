## 2. Product & Technical Knowledge

_This section provides the "source of truth" for technical automation and support._
**2.1. Core Feature Set:** List the top features and the specific problem each one solves.

**1. Website Audit**
Problem solved: Users don’t know about technical/on-page SEO issues on their site. Lack of
SEO knowledge to prioritize issues effectively.
Solution: Automated crawling that detects broken links, duplicate content, meta data issues,
indexing errors, slow pages, etc. Prioritized to-do list tells them which issues to fix first.
**2. Keyword Ranking Monitoring**
Problem solved: Users don’t know how well they are performing in search and if their SEO
efforts have an effect.
Solution: Track keyword positions across countries over time, including positions in Google’s AI


Overviews. Get a detailed picture of your performance for each keyword and landing page.
Track rankings by city or region if you run a local business.

**3. Backlink Monitoring & Analysis**
Problem solved: Users lack visibility into backlink profiles and link building progress. They don’t
know how to find link building opportunities.
Solution: Track backlinks, new/lost and broken links, and anchor text distribution. Analyze
backlink profile quality and get custom link building suggestions.
**4. SEO Competitor Analysis**
Problem solved: users don’t know how they’re performing compared to competitors and what
their competitors are doing differently
Solution: Track competitor rankings and backlinks. Find keyword gaps and refine your own SEO
strategy.
**5. Keyword Research & Content Tools**
Problem solved: Users don’t know how to optimize content for search.
Solution: Find attractive keywords to target with the Keyword Research Tool. Optimize content
relevance with the TF*IDF content optimization tool.
**6. Uptime Monitoring**
Problem solved: Users worry about unnoticed website downtimes that hurt their revenue, UX
and visibility in search
Solution: Seobility automatically tracks website availability and server response times around
the clock, and sends email alerts when issues are detected. Users get information about what
caused the downtimes, so they can fix issues quickly.

**2.4. Product Constraints:** What does the product _not_ do? (Crucial for preventing AI from
over-promising).
Seobility does NOT:
● Fix your website issues for you
● Improve your rankings automatically
● Build backlinks automatically
● Check full page speed (it only measures server response times)
● Connect to Google Search Console
● Provide all the data that advanced SEO tools offer
● Provide built-in content publishing or CMS functionality
● Replace technical developers/SEO experts for advanced issues
It focuses strictly on SEO monitoring, auditing, and optimization guidance.
