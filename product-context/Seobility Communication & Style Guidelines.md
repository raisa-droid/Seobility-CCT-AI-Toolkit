4\. Further guidelines 

Use simple, clear, and concise language ��  
Get to the point and don’t blow up your communication. Make it easy for the reader to understand what you’re saying. Avoid repetitions and unnecessary fluff. 

**Good example** (from the Seobility Beginner’s Guide): 

*Are you new to Seobility and looking to get started quickly? You’re in the right place\!* 

*This guide will help you **take your first steps with Seobility** and **walk you through our complete SEO software suite**.* 

*You’ll learn how to…* 

*● run your first website audit* 

*● track your rankings to monitor your SEO success* 

*● monitor and analyze your backlinks* 

*● analyze the SEO activities of your competitors* 

*● and more\!* 

**Bad example:** 

*Are you new to Seobility and wondering what your first steps should be? Well, you’ve come to the right place. Getting started with a new tool can sometimes feel overwhelming, but don’t worry—we’re here to guide you every step of the way as you begin to explore everything Seobility has to offer.* 

Use beginner-friendly and easy-to-understand language �� 

As noted above, most of our users are not SEO experts. Avoid complex terminology and overly long sentences \- our goal is not to sound smart. Our communication should be understandable with as little cognitive effort as possible. 

**Good example** (Beginner’s Guide)**:** 

*At the top, you’ll find a graph showing your referring domains – that’s the number of unique websites linking to you. You’ll also see statistics about your link profile, including the ratio between follow and nofollow links. This information helps you assess your link profile’s quality.* 

**Bad example:**  
*At the top, you’ll find a graph showing your referring domains and subsequently the distribution of follow and nofollow inbound links.*
