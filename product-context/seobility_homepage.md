## Features

### Website Audit
Automatically crawl your website to detect technical SEO issues such as broken links, duplicate content, and slow-loading pages. Get clear recommendations to improve search engine rankings and AI search visibility.

### Ranking Monitoring
Track keyword rankings and monitor how your visibility changes over time. Identify ranking drops or gains, analyze trends, and understand how optimizations impact your organic search performance.

### Backlink Monitoring
Monitor your backlink profile to see when high-authority domains link to your site, or when valuable links are lost. Discover new link-building opportunities to strengthen off-page SEO.

### Keyword Research & AI Content Tools
Find the right keywords your audience is actually searching for and get clear recommendations on how to optimize your content. Create pages that rank higher and are more likely to appear in AI-generated answers.

Create clear SEO reports including website audits, keyword rankings, and backlink data. Export professional PDF reports with your own logo – ideal for agencies and client reporting.
